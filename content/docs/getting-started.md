# Getting Started with Brainy OS

Welcome to Brainy OS, the institutional operating system for academic excellence and integrity.

## What is Brainy?

Brainy is a comprehensive platform designed to bridge the gap between educational institutions, tutors, and students. Our focus is on providing a secure, transparent, and efficient environment for assessments and academic management.

## Rapid Setup

To get started, follow the guide specific to your role:

### For Students
If you've been invited by an institution or a private tutor, check your email for the invitation link. Once registered, you can immediately begin taking assessments.

### For Tutors
Tutors can create personalized classrooms, invite students, and leverage our AI Assistant to generate high-quality quiz questions in seconds.

### For Institutions
Institutions gain access to faculty management, advanced analytics, and cross-departmental auditing tools. 

## Key Features

- **Integrity Shielding**: Multi-layered anti-cheating protocols.
- **AI Assist**: Smart question generation and grading support.
- **Real-time Monitoring**: Live status of active assessments.
- **Dynamic Grading**: Instant results with detailed performance analytics.
