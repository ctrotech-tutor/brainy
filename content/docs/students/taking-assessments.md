# Taking an Assessment

As a student on Brainy, participating in quizzes is a seamless experience designed to let you focus on your performance.

## Entering an Assessment

1. **Dashboard**: Log in to your student dashboard.
2. **Active Quizzes**: Look for the "Active Assessments" section.
3. **Join**: Click "Join Assessment" on the quiz you intend to take.

## The Integrity Protocol

During the assessment, Brainy monitors several factors to ensure fairness:

- **Browser Locks**: Navigating away from the tab or window may be logged.
- **Time Windows**: Assessments must be completed within the allotted time.
- **Verification**: You may be asked to verify your identity before starting.

## Viewing Results

Once you submit your assessment, your results are processed. Depending on the institution's settings:
- You may see your grade immediately.
- You might receive a detailed performance breakdown.
- Results might be withheld until all students have completed the test.
