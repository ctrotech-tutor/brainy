# Institution Onboarding

Setting up your institution on Brainy is the first step toward a unified academic ecosystem.

## Verification Process

To maintain platform integrity, all institutions must be verified:
1. **Submit Documents**: Provide accreditation papers or official institutional proof.
2. **Admin Review**: Our team will audit the submission within 48 hours.
3. **Approval**: Once approved, your status will change to "Verified".

## Faculty & Department Setup

Organize your institution into logical segments:
- **Faculties**: e.g., Faculty of Science, Faculty of Arts.
- **Departments**: e.g., Computer Science, Biochemistry.
- **Staff Assignment**: Assign deans and department heads with specific permissions.

## Auditing & Analytics

The institutional dashboard provides a high-level overview of:
- **Total Registrations**: Growth of students and staff.
- **Integrity Compliance**: Statistics on flagged activities.
- **Average Performance**: Cross-departmental academic trends.
