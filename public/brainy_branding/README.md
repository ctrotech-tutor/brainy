# Brainy Brand Identity Package

This package contains professional, modern, and production-ready logo concepts for **Brainy**, an educational technology platform.

## Contents

### 1. Primary Logos (`/primary_logos`)
- **brainy_logo_primary.png**: The main brand mark featuring a clean fusion of a brain and a book.
- **brainy_logo_v2.png**: A sleek, ultra-modern variant with an abstract "B" formed by neural nodes.
- **brainy_logo_v3.png**: A professional mark combining a brain silhouette with a checkmark for smart assessments.

### 2. App Icons (`/app_icons`)
- **brainy_app_icon_v1.png**: A bold, recognizable icon for mobile and PWA use.
- **brainy_app_icon_v2.png**: A premium geometric variant for high-end app stores.

### 3. Favicons (`/favicons`)
- **brainy_favicon.png**: A simplified version optimized for browser tabs (16x16 and 32x32).

### 4. Monochrome (`/monochrome`)
- **brainy_logo_monochrome.png**: A pure black/white version for dark mode, certificates, and official reports.

### 5. Institutional Variants (`/institutional_variants`)
- **brainy_institutional_seal.png**: A seal-style logo for university verification and institutional dashboards.

## Brand Colors
- **Primary Purple**: #8B5CF6 (Vibrant, academic, and modern)
- **Secondary**: #F3F4F6 (Clean and professional)

## Design Philosophy
The designs focus on **intelligence, structure, and credibility**, avoiding childish mascots in favor of a premium SaaS aesthetic suitable for universities and professional institutions.
